HOW TO ADD YOUR PERSONAL PHOTOGRAPHS:

1. Copy your 5 personal photos into this folder: `public/photos/`
2. Name them:
   - photo1.jpg (or photo1.png / photo1.webp)
   - photo2.jpg
   - photo3.jpg
   - photo4.jpg
   - photo5.jpg
3. The website will automatically detect and display your real photographs with the captions:
   1. "A moment I wish I could relive ❤️"
   2. "My favourite smile."
   3. "Every memory with you is special."
   4. "You make ordinary moments beautiful."
   5. "My favourite person, always."

The gallery preserves original aspect ratios and never crops faces.
Clicking any photo opens it in the romantic lightbox!
